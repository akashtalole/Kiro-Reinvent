# Product: Kiro Reinvent Labs

A free GitHub Pages site (https://akashtalole.github.io/Kiro-Reinvent/) of hands-on labs, use cases, scenarios and reference architectures for building end-to-end solutions with Kiro (IDE, CLI, Web, Crew), AWS MCP servers, Strands Agents and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.

- **Audience:** builders, platform teams and leaders. Labs are runnable; docs are decision aids.
- **Voice:** plain English, short sentences, active voice. Say what something does, then how. No marketing adjectives.
- **Truthfulness beats completeness.** Every product fact links an official source. Mark preview features. If a fact can't be verified, leave it out or say it's unverified. Productivity numbers are labelled as self-reported.
- **Safety by default.** Labs are least-privilege, read-only first, and never deploy to production from an agent without a human gate. Anything that bills has a Clean up section.
